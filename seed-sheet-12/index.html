How this card works

Your 12-word recovery phrase is spread over three cards. Each card holds 8 of the 12 words. Any two cards together contain all 12; one card alone does not.

Card Awords 1–8
Card Bwords 5–12
Card Cwords 1–4, 9–12 (this card)
  1. Write each word next to its number. Wrong numbering breaks recovery.
  2. Fill all three cards, then restore the wallet from two of them to prove it works before funding it.
  3. Store the cards in three separate places. Never photograph them.

12 words are a weaker split than 24. Someone holding one card knows 8 words and only has to guess 4 (about a trillion candidates after the checksum). That is within reach of a serious attacker with GPUs. Treat a lone card as sensitive, add a BIP39 passphrase, or use SLIP-39 for large balances.
