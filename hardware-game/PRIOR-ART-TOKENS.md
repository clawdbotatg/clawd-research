# Prior Art: Skill-Weighted Pot Redistribution Wrapped in a Token

Design under evaluation: ~$100 USDC buy-ins → non-transferable credits → pooled reserve/bonding curve → deterministic resource-management gameplay → burn commodities to mint a final token → sell-only against the curve during the round → fully liquid post-round.

---

## 1. FOMO3D & Ponzi-transparent games (2018)

**Mechanism.** Team Just's FOMO3D (July 2018), self-billed as "the world's first legitimate exit scam": buy keys with ETH at a rising price; each buy feeds the pot, streams dividends pro-rata to key holders (the PoWH3D inheritance — 10% tax on every buy/sell paid to incumbents), and adds 30s to a countdown timer. Timer hits zero → last buyer takes ~48% of pot.

**What happened.** Peak ~$12M at stake; ~69,629 ETH inflow on the peak day (July 21), visibly congesting Ethereum L1 ([HTX Research](https://medium.com/huobi-research/huobi-focal-point-vol-9-fomo3d-angel-or-devil-9e77d7df0a7)). Round 1 ended Aug 22, 2018 when the winner executed a **block-stuffing attack**: pre-deployed contracts fired 5,000+ high-fee txs that `assert()`-burned 4.2M gas each iff the attacker was still last-buyer, filling ~11 consecutive blocks so nobody could buy a key before the timer expired. Spent >4 ETH in fees; won **10,469.66 ETH (~$2.2–3M)** — no miner collusion needed, just fee-priority ordering ([SECBIT forensics](https://secbit.io/blog/en/2018/08/23/how-the-winner-got-fomo3d-prize-a-detailed-explanation/), [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/fomo3d-winner-clogged-ethereum-blockchain/), [Solmaz's anatomy](https://solmaz.io/2018/10/18/anatomy-block-stuffing/)). Round 3 drew ~103 ETH — a ~99% falloff; transparent extraction sells exactly one cohort.

**Portable lessons.** (a) Any discrete "last actor before deadline wins X" endgame is an auction for block ordering, not a game — post-PBS a searcher just bids a bundle ([Flash Boys 2.0](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.05234)). Settle round-end via a batch/window at one clearing price. (b) Transparency-as-marketing works once; retention needs the game itself. (c) Dividend drips from newcomers to incumbents = the Forsage prosecution pattern — burn-to-mint avoids it; keep it that way.

## 2. Words3 / Small Brain Games — the closest deployed precedent

**Mechanism.** Onchain Scrabble on MUD; letters priced by a GDA/VRGDA-family curve (price jumps per purchase, decays with idle blocks — [words3-contracts](https://github.com/smallbraingames/words3-contracts)). All ETH → onchain `Treasury`. **`ClaimSystem.claim(points)` is literally this design:** burn points → redeem `treasury × points/totalPoints`, claims gated until `claimRestrictionBlock` (round end), `feeBps` rake at claim, per-address spend cap + whitelist knobs. May 2024 Redstone relaunch made it perpetual — sell points anytime ([announcement](https://x.com/0xsmallbrain/status/1790960226260443271)).

**What happened.** Round 1 (Nov 2022): 6 days, 100 players, 1,212 words. Round 2 (Base, Aug 2023): 4 days, **122 players, $22,423 total spend; winner saucepoint.eth netted +$1,379 (6.1% of pot) by scripting against the public Merkle dictionary**, beating #2 by $23.85; ~85%+ of players lost money ([WASD primer](https://wasd.mirror.xyz/sfPq0Noc0mIpBZ3q8APnURXaxbaRNz9_E6vi5t2SzO8)). 2024 community rounds went to **15 minutes with 0.5 ETH caps**; Small Brain shipped a public botkit (embrace, don't fight). The studio went quiet mid-2024; the contracts outlived the hosting cadence.

**Portable lessons.** The burn-commodity-for-pro-rata-pot claim is proven in ~40 lines of Solidity — steal the shape. But: zero-sum-minus-rake means the median player is the yield and knows it; **round length is the biggest fun/degeneracy dial** (15-min pandemonium fun, 4-day rounds become a botted job); early entrants on a cheap-start curve collect structural rent; with open state + deterministic rules, **scripting wins** — cap, whitelist, or embrace. And the thing that dies first is round-hosting, not code.

Adjacent: **Sky Strife** — fixed 100M 🔮 supply, 100 🔮 per match, winners paid from a self-throttling pool; Lattice ultimately dropped seasons for unmanned "Autonomous Mode" ([blog](https://lattice.xyz/blog/sky-strife-autonomous-mode)) — per-match buy-in is sybil-resistant because skill is verified per match. **Loot Survivor** — 25 $LORDS per run streaming to top-3 leaderboard; pivoted in 2.0 to token buybacks, suggesting pure redistribution didn't sustain ([docs](https://docs.provable.games/lootsurvivor/token)).

## 3. Dark Forest & 0xMonaco — prizes vs tokens

**Dark Forest.** Free/whitelisted entry, fixed dev-funded prizes: **1024 DAI to top 15** in v0.3–v0.5 ([v0.3 rules](https://blog.zkga.me/v3-rules), [v0.5](https://blog.zkga.me/dark-forest-v05)); v0.6 switched to 63 Valhalla planet NFTs per round; R5 drew ~2,000 players/10 days ([wrapup](https://blog.zkga.me/v6-r5-wrapup)). Deliberately no token: no price to defend, no securities surface, and rules could change every round — impossible once a token capitalizes expectations. DAO squads (Orden GG 1.7B pts) showed **collusion beats individuals under additive scoring** ([Naavik](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/dark-forest-beacon-of-light/)). Cost: prizes stayed 4-figure, and rounds ended when the host stopped hosting.

**0xMonaco** (Paradigm CTF 2022). Player-written car contracts race; each car gets a **closed, non-transferable 15,000-coin budget**; ACCELERATE/SHELL priced by true VRGDAs ([Monaco.sol](https://github.com/soliditee/paradigm-ctf-2022-0xmonaco/blob/main/src/Monaco.sol), [VRGDA paper](https://www.paradigm.xyz/2022/08/vrgda)). 127 teams; meta evolved live over 30h (race-from-behind conservation beat early spend); MatchboxDAO's Battle of Titans reran it with new items to break the meta, Uniswap winning ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/206516/uniswap-wins-web3-gaming-battle-of-titans-competition)). **Lesson:** a fixed non-withdrawable budget + VRGDA-priced actions yields deep strategy with two constants — and because coins never touched money, iterating the ruleset was free. *Every unit of "realness" in a currency is a unit of design freedom lost.* Prize = reputation was enough for elite grinding. The non-transferable in-game credits are the 0xMonaco layer; keep them maximally fake so only the terminal token is real.

## 4. Axie SLP / StepN GST — uncapped emission death spirals

**Axie.** SLP: uncapped, gameplay-minted; only real sink was breeding — a sink whose volume depends on *new-player growth*. Peak $0.399 (Jul 2021) → ~$0.001 (−99.7%); minting ran ~2:1 over burn (~300M/day minted); Feb 2022's Season 20 zeroed adventure/quest SLP (−56% emission, "drastic and decisive action now or… total and permanent economic collapse" — [official post](https://blog.axieinfinity.com/p/upcoming-season-20-and-economic-balancing)); scholar earnings fell below the PH minimum wage ([Naavik/Doucet deconstruction](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/axie-infinity-deconstruction)); then the $625M Ronin hack ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2022/03/29/axie-infinitys-ronin-network-suffers-625m-exploit)). Doucet's carnival test: *"attendees must pay $5 for the Ferris wheel and not expect $6 when they leave."*

**StepN.** GST ATH ~$9 (Apr 28, 2022) → −98% in two months; sneaker floor $1,571 → $88; DAU ~705K → thousands; China GPS ban (July 15, 2022) was accelerant, not cause ([Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/stepn-rise-fall-future/), [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/101510/stepn-move-earn-cryptocurrencies-plummet-gameplay-blocked-china)). The capped GMT beside uncapped GST capped nothing — as users left, sneakers-per-DAU rose 3→12+, so **per-capita emission rose as the game shrank**, and GMT's burn utility switched off exactly when GST broke ([Vader Research, called in advance](https://defivader.medium.com/why-stepns-collapse-is-inevitable-5259a6584a98)).

**Portable lesson — the accounting identity.** Faucet indexed to player *count*, sink indexed to player *growth* → net emission goes structurally positive the moment growth flattens; no tuning schedule survives the inflection. **Sinks denominated in the emitted token are lagged faucets, not sinks.** This design inverts that by construction: mint is capped by reserve backing ex ante, the commodity has no external price to spiral, and the worst case is a redemption drain of known maximum depth, not a spiral. The residual: without an earn subsidy, gameplay has to actually be fun.

## 5. Bonding curves & one-way valves

- **friend.tech** — quadratic curve (`price = n²/16000 ETH`), contract as sole counterparty, 10% fees. $2M/day peak fees, ~$52M TVL, ~$18M+ protocol revenue; −90% activity in months, FRIEND $3→$0.08, contracts renounced to null address Sept 2024 ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/315172/friend-tech-team-renounces-control-of-smart-contracts-following-stagnant-growth)). *Lesson:* two-way convex curves make the marginal buyer the product; the sell-only phase removes that failure mode — the risk moves to the post-round liquidity cliff. 10% friction didn't save it.
- **pump.fun** — virtual-reserve constant-product curve → graduation at ~$69k mcap, ~$12k SOL + tokens seeded into a burned-key LP. ~12.4M launches, **~1.2% graduate**, ~$1.2B cumulative revenue ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/coindesk-news/2025/12/10/most-influential-pump-fun)). *Lesson:* hard pre-announced graduation works technically, and the transition moment is the single most gamed point — expect players to optimize burn timing purely for the unlock; **seed post-round liquidity from the reserve so day-one depth is known in advance.**
- **Zora coins** — 20% of trading fees locked as permanent pool liquidity ([docs](https://docs.zora.co/coins)): a fee-funded one-way liquidity ratchet worth copying.
- **One-way valve precedents:** **Blast** ($2.3B locked deposits, violent unlock, maximal criticism — [Blockworks](https://blockworks.com/news/blast-optimistic-rollup-launch)) is the inverse and the talking point: exit-always-open is far more defensible. **Astroport lockdrop/LBA** shipped literal withdraw-only sub-phases with step-down rights ([Delphi](https://members.delphidigital.io/reports/introducing-lockdrop-lba-a-novel-token-launch-mechanism/)). **Hegic IBCO** — everyone settles at one uniform final price, killing intra-phase timing games ([announcement](https://medium.com/hegic/join-hegic-initial-bonding-curve-offering-d1746a32a552)). **Balancer LBPs** — sell-pressure-dominant price discovery as a *feature* ([docs](https://docs.balancer.fi/concepts/explore-available-balancer-pools/liquidity-bootstrapping-pool/liquidity-bootstrapping-pool.html)). **OlympusDAO** — $1,415 → ~$32, price fell *through* "backing" because backing was narrative, not a redemption right ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2022/01/11/olympus-tanks-30-led-by-liquidations-on-fuse-souring-market-sentiment)); same for Wonderland TIME. *The invariant to publish live:* `reserve ≥ outstanding supply × curve sell price` — the sell-only curve is a **hard redemption right**, the thing OHM/TIME faked.
- **True sell-only-curve ancestors:** essentially no branded DeFi precedent; the real ones are **Moloch ragequit** (burn shares for exact pro-rata treasury slice, battle-tested since 2019 — [mechanism page](https://gitcoin.co/mechanisms/molochdao)) and Vitalik's **DAICO**. Cautionary tail: Nouns fork arbitrageurs bought in *just to ragequit* (~$27M). Safe iff buying-to-redeem is impossible — which burn-only minting gives for free. The arb then migrates to the commodity layer: **commodity → token → curve-USDC must never exceed the commodity's acquisition cost**, or players farm mechanically instead of playing. Bonus property: every early exit below final value raises reserve-per-remaining-token — early exits ratchet the floor *up* for holders, Moloch-style. Say so loudly.

## 6. Legality landscape (flags, not advice)

**DFS carve-out.** DraftKings/FanDuel lived on UIGEA 2006's fantasy exemption — prizes "established and made known in advance" and "not determined by the number of participants or fees" — plus state skill tests: predominance (majority), material-element (NY), any-chance (strictest). The 2015 NY AG fight ended in a licensing statute + $6M settlements; ~30 states followed; HI/ID/MT/WA/NV remain off-limits ([state list](https://www.legalsportsreport.com/dfs-sites/legal-states/)). *Humphrey v. Viacom*'s entry-fee-≠-bet factors: fee unconditional, **prize fixed in advance regardless of entry count**, no house stake, skill predominates ([brief](https://www.quimbee.com/cases/humphrey-v-viacom)). **Our pot IS the pooled entries and scales with entrants — the wrong side of the key factor.** Offsets: fully deterministic gameplay (stronger than DFS) and no house rake.

**Gambling vs skill.** Consideration + chance + prize; kill one element. Deterministic play attacks chance, but material-element/any-chance states and percentage-game states (CA, FL) attack the **entry-funded pool itself** regardless of skill ([esports law guide](https://www.kisacoresearch.com/sites/default/files/documents/esports-download_ifrah.pdf)). Buy-in esports tournaments are tolerated in practice, not precedent. *Kent v. PoolTogether* was dismissed on standing only — no safe harbor ([Blockworks](https://blockworks.com/news/pooltogether-class-action-dismissed)).

**Howey on the post-round token.** "Value from your own gameplay" helps during the round (same own-efforts logic as the SEC's Feb 2025 [meme-coin statement](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/staff-statement-meme-coins)) — but post-round, **non-player buyers have pure profit expectation against a pooled fund**, the pattern *Friel v. Dapper Labs* survived dismissal on (settled $4M, 2024 — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/298286/dapper-labs-settles-nba-top-shot-moments-lawsuit-for-4-million)); note Dapper's alleged *control over cash-out* rhymes with a sell-only phase. Tailwind: Atkins-SEC "Project Crypto," proposed Regulation Crypto Assets safe harbor, CLARITY Act pending Senate cloture Sept 15, 2026 ([CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/08/08/u-s-senate-opens-first-stage-of-crypto-clarity-act-voting-to-give-bill-a-chance-next-month)) — none of which touches private class actions or state regulators.

**Sweepstakes silhouette.** "Buy USDC → non-transferable credits → redeemable token" pattern-matches the Gold Coin/Sweeps Coin dual-currency model that ~17 states have banned or restricted in 2025–26 (CA AB 831, IL's 65 cease-and-desists, six operators dead since Oct 2025 — [roundup](https://www.gamblinginsider.com/news/99597/us-sweepstakes-casinos-2025-market-contraction)). Differentiate loudly; geofence banned states from day one.

## 7. Season-token design

- **Fresh-token-per-round precedent is thin but the physics favor it.** Crypto Unicorns' persistent UNIM/RBW ended in a de facto reset anyway (XAI migration, 10:1 RBW→CU — a new token wearing continuity's clothes, with all the bagholder resentment, [PlayToEarn](https://playtoearn.com/news/crypto-unicorns-migrates-to-rbw-token-becomes-cu)). **TreasureDAO MAGIC**, the shared-token maximal case, concentrated every game's balance debt into one asset: treasury depletion disclosed Apr 2025, chain shut down May 30, 2025 ([The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/nfts-and-web3/treasure-dao-restructures-and-cut-costs-in-attempt-to-survive)). Correlated failure is the tax on shared persistence.
- **The hybrid that worked: Aavegotchi** — one persistent GHST (itself originally DAI-bonding-curve-issued), seasonal Rarity Farming competitions paid from **recycled revenue (40% of in-game spend), never fresh emission** ([docs](https://docs.aavegotchi.com/own/earning-in-aavegotchi/rarity-farming)); 8+ seasons, no death spiral. Seasonal *pools* vs seasonal *tokens* is a real middle path.
- **Epoch-settled claims are proven infra**: Ribbon/Theta weekly vault rounds — the round token is closer to a vault share than to SLP ([docs](https://docs.ribbon.finance/theta-vault/theta-vault/options-settlement)). **Art Gobblers GOO** is the counter-lesson: Paradigm-grade mechanism design (−98%) — sophistication doesn't substitute for capping issuance against exogenous value ([GOO paper](https://www.paradigm.xyz/2022/09/goo), [post-mortem](https://medium.com/the-brr/the-rise-and-fall-of-art-gobblers-a403f819d6de)).
- **Trade-offs:** per-round token = balance-sheet amnesty, honest poker-tournament accounting, quarantined balancing errors, a real terminal state — at the cost of zero compounding liquidity/brand and **N rounds = N token-generation events = N fresh Howey/gambling looks** (mitigant: identical factory bytecode; the redeemable-chip shape). The survivor invariant across the whole list: **rewards were always claims on money already inside the system, never fresh supply whose value needed the next cohort.** This design has it by construction.

## Top 5 failure modes to engineer around (ranked)

1. **Regulatory shape: entry-funded pot + winner-take-most + a post-round liquid token is simultaneously the classic wagering fact pattern (fails Humphrey's fixed-prize factor), the Dapper "controlled cash-out" Howey pattern, and the sweeps-casino dual-currency silhouette in the middle of a 17-state crackdown.** Mitigations that fall out of the record: no house rake from the pot, deterministic open-source rules, geofence/KYC the hostile states, consider redeem-and-burn (fixed-value claim) instead of free-floating post-round trading — or delay/structure liquidity — and keep the chip framing, not the investment framing.
2. **The endgame boundary is MEV bait.** FOMO3D round 1 was won with a $1,000 gas bribe; pump.fun graduations get sniped; every deterministic round-end block invites ordering games (last exit at the best reserve ratio, first buy at unlock). Batch all round-final actions at one uniform clearing price (Hegic IBCO-style) and pre-seed the post-round pool from the reserve so the unlock has known depth instead of a cliff.
3. **Bots and collusion harvest the median player.** Words3's winner scripted the public dictionary for a 6.1% edge while 85% lost; Dark Forest's additive scoring was dominated by DAO squads; sybils defeat per-address caps. Deterministic + open-state means scripting *will* win — decide explicitly (embrace with a botkit, per-match skill verification à la Sky Strife, whitelists, short rounds) and design scoring so collusion doesn't compound.
4. **Commodity-layer arbitrage breaks the burn valve.** The reserve cap makes token emission safe, but if commodity → token → curve-USDC ever yields more than commodity acquisition cost (or if anything lets you buy-to-immediately-redeem, the Nouns-fork drain), play collapses into mechanical farming. Keep in-game credits non-transferable and price the mint so the curve floor never exceeds marginal production cost — 0xMonaco's VRGDA-priced closed budget is the template for the inner economy.
5. **The round cadence dies before the contracts do.** Words3, Dark Forest, Sky Strife seasons, Small Brain itself: every pot-round game's actual failure was hosting/occasion burnout, not mechanism failure — and zero-sum-minus-rake gloom (median player = yield) accelerates it. Keep rounds short and event-shaped, make the gameplay carry fun without an earn subsidy (Doucet's carnival test), and budget for round operations as the real product.
