# Pico hats

A catalog of add-on boards a Raspberry Pi Pico plugs straight into.

- Page: https://claude.ai/artifact/6nWu46CT7XmQAEgrDuUDXD
- `hats.json` is the data. `python3 build.py` rebuilds `index.html` from it and `template.html`.
- Compiled 2026-11-09 from vendor sites. Boards with their own RP2040 are left out.

Known gaps:
- Pimoroni's shop rate-limited the search, so its list (22) may be missing older packs and most statuses are unknown.
- Indie entries have prices from search snippets, not product pages.
- Spotpear entries are likely Waveshare boards under another listing.
- Cytron and MikroElektronika blocked fetches; their prices are from search results.
